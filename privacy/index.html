TryPagent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

TryPagent is a private content tool operated by Ahmed Saad, an individual ("we"). It prepares photo posts for the operator's own social media accounts. Every post is reviewed and approved by a person before anything is sent. For TikTok, approved photo posts are sent to the operator's own TikTok account inbox, and the operator finishes and publishes them manually in the TikTok app. TryPagent does not publish to TikTok directly. It is not offered to the public.

Who uses TryPagent

Only the operator and the TikTok account the operator connects. No other people sign in, and TryPagent does not collect information about other TikTok users.

What we receive from TikTok

  • When the operator authorizes TryPagent with TikTok Login Kit, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token, their expiry times, the granted scopes and the account's open_id.
  • Scopes requested: video.upload (to send photo posts to the account's TikTok inbox) and user.info.basic (granted with Login Kit; TryPagent does not call TikTok's user information API).
  • After each upload, TikTok returns an upload ID and its processing status, which TryPagent checks.
  • We do not access followers, messages, analytics, other users' data or existing posts.

What we send to TikTok

  • Approved photos (as public HTTPS links that TikTok downloads), the post caption with hashtags, and a short title (the product name).

How we use the data

The tokens are used only to send approved photo posts to the connected account's inbox and to check the status of those uploads. Nothing is published by TryPagent; publishing happens only when the operator completes the post in the TikTok app.

Where data is stored

  • TikTok tokens are stored in a local file on the operator's computer, not in a database, chat or logs; log output masks them. The access token is renewed automatically shortly before it expires; a new refresh token replaces the old one.
  • The account's open_id, upload IDs, statuses and published post links are stored in a local database on the operator's computer.
  • Images to be posted are stored at public HTTPS addresses in the operator's Supabase Storage so that TikTok can download them.

Third-party services

  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API) — receives the photos, captions and titles the operator approved.
  • Supabase — stores generated and approved images at public URLs, and saves content records.
  • OpenAI — writes captions and edits product images using public product information. No TikTok account data is sent to OpenAI.
  • Tavily and Apify — public web search and public trend information used to research products. No TikTok account data is sent to them.
  • Telegram — the operator's private review chat; it shows previews and status, never tokens.
  • Instagram (Meta) and Buffer — used separately to publish to the operator's Instagram account and, as an alternative route, to remind the operator to publish on TikTok. TryPagent's TikTok tokens are not shared with them.

Retention and deletion

  • TikTok tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the account. To disconnect: revoke TryPagent's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions, or the current equivalent) and delete the local token file. After that, no further uploads are possible.
  • Images on Supabase Storage are kept until they are deleted manually. TryPagent does not delete them automatically.
  • Upload records (open_id, upload IDs, statuses and post links) are kept in the local database until they are deleted manually.
  • To ask for deletion of data related to your TikTok account, contact us at the address below.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell TikTok data and do not share it with anyone except the services listed above, for the purposes described.

Children

TryPagent is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.
