TryPagent

An internal content workflow that one creator uses to prepare, review and publish photo posts about products for his own social media accounts.

TryPagent is operated by Ahmed Saad, an individual. It is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no paid plan and no other users.

How it works

  1. Request. The operator starts a request in his private Telegram chat and chooses the product category, country, content type (for example a review or a comparison), number of products and target account. Nothing starts before he confirms.
  2. Research. TryPagent finds products from public web results and public trend information, checks each product's official page and image, and skips products already posted on the same account.
  3. Writing and images. Captions and slide text are written with AI in a fixed persona voice, from documented facts only. Product photos may be edited with AI to place the real product in a consistent setting. The text is then written on the images locally.
  4. Review. The operator sees every image that will be published, in order, with the full caption, and can edit, regenerate or reject. Only an approved version can be scheduled.
  5. Scheduling. The operator chooses the platform and time. Instagram posts are published automatically at that time. TikTok posts are sent to the TikTok app for the operator to finish (see below).
  6. Result. The post link is recorded, and the product is not posted again on the same account for the same content type.

TikTok integration

  • Login Kit (desktop): the operator connects his own TikTok account once from his computer. TikTok asks for user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • Content Posting API — Upload: an approved photo post is sent to the account's TikTok inbox (MEDIA_UPLOAD, photos downloaded by TikTok from a verified URL prefix).
  • Finished by a person: the operator opens the inbox notification, reviews the post in TikTok's editor, and publishes it himself. TryPagent never publishes to TikTok directly (Direct Post is not used).

What TryPagent does not do

  • It does not publish to TikTok directly or post without the operator's approval.
  • It does not sign in other users, post to accounts that did not authorize it, or comment, follow, like or send messages.
  • It does not sell or share data beyond the services listed in the Privacy Policy.

Data

TikTok access tokens stay in a local file on the operator's computer and are never shown in chat or logs. Images to be posted are stored at public addresses on the operator's Supabase Storage so that TikTok and Instagram can download them. Data is kept until it is deleted manually.
