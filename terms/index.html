TryPagent — Terms of Service

Last updated: November 1, 2026

These terms apply to TryPagent, a private content tool operated by Ahmed Saad, an individual, for the operator's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no paid plans.

What TryPagent does

  • Prepares photo posts (images and captions) and shows them for review in the operator's private Telegram chat.
  • After the operator approves and schedules a post, sends it to the connected TikTok account's inbox. The post is not public until the operator opens it in the TikTok app and finishes publishing it there.
  • Never publishes to TikTok directly, never posts to accounts that have not authorized it, and does not interact with other users (no comments, follows, likes or messages).

Your account

  • Connecting a TikTok account requires signing in with TikTok and approving the requested permissions.
  • You can disconnect at any time by revoking access in the TikTok app; uploads stop after that.
  • You are responsible for what you choose to publish and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Content

Captions and images are prepared from public product information and are reviewed by the operator before any upload. Images may be edited with AI. The operator decides how to finish and disclose each post in TikTok's editor when publishing.

Availability

TryPagent is provided as is, for the operator's own use. Uploads can fail, for example because of TikTok limits or an expired authorization. Failures are reported in the review chat, and an upload whose result is unknown is not repeated automatically.
